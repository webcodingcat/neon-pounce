# Neon Pounce

_Think fast!_

Neon Pounce is a fast sorting puzzle for phones. A segment lights up in the
centre. Its position is fixed, you only choose the ring: tap one of the six
rings to drop the segment there before its timer runs out. A full ring clears
and scores, a ring of a single colour scores three times as much.

The game is in development. What you can play today is an early version with
placeholder graphics and without sound.

## Play

Open **<https://webcodingcat.github.io/neon-pounce/>** on your phone and hold it
upright.

- **Tap a ring** to place the segment at its position in that ring.
- **Swipe the segment out of the centre** to throw it away. You have a few
  discards. With none left, a swipe gives the segment up and costs a life.
- A tap on a ring whose slot is taken costs time.
- When the timer runs out you lose one of three lives.
- With the last life the game is over and shows your score. "Again" starts a
  new game.

The game is made for Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. It needs no
account and sends no data anywhere.

## Put it on your home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android (Chrome):** open the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home screen".

For now this is a shortcut to the web page and needs an internet connection.
Installing the game as an app that also runs offline is planned.
